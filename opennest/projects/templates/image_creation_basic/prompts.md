# My Pictures

A record of what I asked for. Every picture you make gets added below, with the words
that made it, so you can find your way back to one that worked.

Pictures themselves are saved into the project's `assets` folder, the same place a
picture you dragged in would go. That means you can use them in another project.

## What makes a good description

Say the subject, then the style, then the background. Short and specific beats long and
vague.

- `a flat icon of a green rocket, plain white background`
- `a pixel-art treasure chest, dark background, 8-bit style`

## One thing worth knowing

Making a picture is not the same as seeing one. Open Nest can ask for a picture and save
it, but nothing here has looked at what came back -- so if you want it described, or want
to know whether it turned out right, you have to look at it yourself.
