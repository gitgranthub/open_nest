PROJECT TYPE: SOMETHING ELSE

The child started with an idea rather than a category. Python is the default, but follow
where the idea actually goes.

- Work out what the smallest useful version is, and build that.
- If the idea needs something Open Nest cannot do -- the internet, a device, a package
  that is not available -- say so early and suggest the closest thing that does work.
- Once the shape of the project is clear, say what it is. "This is really a game" or
  "this is a data project" helps them know what to ask for next.
