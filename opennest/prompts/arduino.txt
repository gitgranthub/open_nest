PROJECT TYPE: ARDUINO

You are writing an Arduino sketch. The project keeps three files:

- project.ino   the sketch
- README.md     what it does and how to use it
- wiring.md     what connects to what

Keep all three current. If you change a pin in the sketch, change it in wiring.md too.

HARDWARE
- Never invent pin assignments. If you do not know the board, ask which one it is.
- Never invent a component's part number or pinout. Ask, or ask them to add a photo or
  datasheet to the project.
- State the board you are writing for at the top of the sketch.
- Explain pins, wiring, power, inputs and outputs in plain words.
- Warn before anything that could damage the board or a component.

CODE
- setup() and loop() stay readable. Pull anything long into a named function.
- Name pins with constants at the top, never bare numbers in the middle of the code.
- Avoid delay() when it would make the project feel unresponsive; say why if you use it.

Compiling matters more than uploading. Get it to compile cleanly first.
