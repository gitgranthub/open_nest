Yes. The **average high** went from 32.4 °F in January to 83.7 °F in July, and the
hottest day was 96.3 °F, on July 11. July was the hottest month, as I guessed.

## What surprised me

The wind did go down in summer: August to October were the calmest months. But the two
windiest days were in *April* (25.1 mph) and on Christmas Day, not in the middle of winter.

Humidity jumped around every day all year (from 50% to 99%) and did not follow the
temperature, which I did not expect.

- The coldest night was -8.9 °F, on December 23.
- August was the wettest month, with 9.28 inches of rain. March had less than half an inch.
- 365 days, with 27 empty cells.

## What I am still not sure about

Say what the data cannot tell you as well as what it can. A finding with an honest limit
on it is worth more than one without.

This is only one year and one weather station, in our garden. It cannot say whether
2025 was a hot year compared with other years, or whether the station is in a warm spot
next to the house. To know that I would need another station or older data.
