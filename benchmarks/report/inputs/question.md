# The question

What are you trying to find out?

Write it as a question you could actually answer, not a topic. "Does it rain more in
March than in May?" is answerable. "Weather" is not.

## My question

Was the summer of 2025 at our house really hotter than the rest of the year, and did
the wind and the humidity change with it?

## What I think the answer is

<!-- A guess before you look is worth writing down. It is more interesting when you
     turn out to be wrong. -->
I think July was the hottest month and that it was also the least windy, because the
hot days felt still.
